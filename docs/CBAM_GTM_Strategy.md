# MatDataHub: Lean Go-To-Market (GTM) Strategy

*This document outlines the marketing and user-acquisition strategy for the CBAM Analytics Engine, specifically designed for a bootstrapped launch without a legal team or an existing audience.*

## 1. Product Positioning (The "No Lawyer" Approach)
Because the platform does not currently have formal sign-off from a trade lawyer, **it should not be marketed as tax-filing software.** 

Instead, position it as a **Strategic Planning & Cost Estimation Tool**.
* **Target Audience:** Procurement Managers, Supply Chain Analysts, and Sourcing Directors.
* **The Pitch:** "Don't get blindsided by the 2026 EU Carbon Tax. Estimate your hidden CBAM liabilities today so you can negotiate better rates with your suppliers tomorrow."
* **Why this works:** Procurement teams need fast, directional data to make sourcing decisions. They don't need legal tax precision; they need a reliable estimate.

---

## 2. The "Cold Start" Solution: Demo Data
When new users visit a data tool, they rarely have a perfectly formatted CSV ready to upload. To solve this, the app includes a **"Try with Demo Data"** feature on the upload screen.

**Available Demos:**
1. **Automotive BOM:** Highlights a mix of CBAM-covered goods (Steel, Aluminium) and exempt goods (Plastics) to show how the engine filters scope.
2. **Construction BOM:** Highlights massive tonnages of Cement and Rebar, showcasing how the engine handles large financial liabilities and indirect emissions.

*Use these buttons during live demos to instantly showcase the dashboard's value without needing to prepare a file.*

## 3. Content Marketing Playbook (Zero Audience Required)

When you are ready to launch (or when you secure your custom domain), use this playbook to generate your first leads.

### A. The Loom Video Strategy
Record a 2-3 minute screen-share video using Loom (or similar).
* **0:00 - 0:30 (The Hook):** "If you import steel or aluminium into the EU, your costs are going up in 2026. Here is how you calculate exactly how much."
* **0:30 - 1:30 (The Demo):** Click the "Automotive BOM" demo button on your site. Watch the dashboard populate. 
* **1:30 - 2:00 (The Breakdown):** Point out the specific financial liability on the screen. "As you can see, this 50-tonne steel shipment from China immediately flags €1,200 in hidden CBAM tax because of their grid emissions."
* **2:00 - 2:30 (The Ask):** Offer a free audit (see section B).

### B. Social Media Templates
Post the Loom video on LinkedIn and relevant supply chain subreddits/forums using these templates:

**Post Template 1 (Fear/Urgency):**
> Procurement managers: Do you know what your hidden EU carbon tax liability is for 2026? 
> 
> The CBAM definitive period is going to wipe out margins on imported steel, aluminium, and cement if you aren't prepared. I built an engine that calculates this liability instantly based on EU Commission default values. 
> 
> Watch me run an automotive chassis BOM through it in 3 seconds. 👇
> 
> If you want to see the impact on your specific supply chain, send me a DM and I'll run your anonymized BOM through my engine for free.

**Post Template 2 (Strategic/Value):**
> Stop negotiating blind with your overseas suppliers. 
> 
> If you are importing to the EU, a cheap supplier with high carbon emissions will actually cost you MORE in 2026 due to CBAM taxes. I built a tool that takes your standard Bill of Materials and instantly calculates your true landed cost including carbon taxes.
> 
> [Video Link]

## 4. Lead Generation: The "Free Audit" Funnel
Until you have a highly polished, self-serve SaaS funnel on a custom domain, do things that don't scale:
1. When someone replies to your post or sends a DM, ask them to send an anonymized CSV with just `Material Name`, `Weight`, and `Country of Origin`.
2. Run it through your Render app yourself.
3. Export the resulting CSV or take screenshots of the dashboard.
4. Send them a personalized mini-report. 
5. Ask for their feedback on the tool and if they'd be willing to pay a monthly subscription for direct access. 

This guarantees you get high-quality test data, validates your pricing model, and secures your first beta testers.
